# Screen metaphor recipes and how to build the details

**Recipes**
- **Field dossier (collection index, bestiary, lore):** a cream paper card with faint noise, tape strips across its corners, a rotated red completion stamp, a polaroid of the area, stat cards drawn as small monitors, specimens as taped polaroid cards with a rarity pill and "???" silhouettes for undiscovered ones, a sticky note for the set bonus, a ticket-shaped reward card with a locked twin. A list of areas on the left picks the file.
- **Transit terminal (travel, chapters, dungeon floors):** a metro-style route line (solid = open, dashed = locked) through numbered stop discs, the current stop in gold with a marker for the player's position, and status pills per stop (the missing requirement in red). Beside it a CRT screen showing the selected destination, with knob dials, a speaker grille, an LCD stop counter and a big skewed GO button.
- **Tune-up bench (upgrades, stats, forge):** tall cards coloured by stat type with a tab label, big icon, the effect per level, a gauge dial with the level, the next gain and a price button; a locked card that says more is coming.
- **Curio cabinet (items, materials):** category chips with counts, rarity-gradient tiles with tiny corner labels, NEW ribbons, gold brackets on the selection, and an inspect panel with a rarity banner, showcase, owned count, where it drops, and delete and locate buttons.
- **Settings console:** a numbered section list on the left with icons, grouped cards, slider rows with a percentage, toggles, segmented choices, keybind chips, and a one-line hint under any control the server clamps.
