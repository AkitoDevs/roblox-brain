# Screen patterns for shops, rewards and progression

- **Shop:** featured banner first (art, NEW or LIMITED tag, countdown, price buttons with a gift button beside each), then category grids. Best-value tag on the bundle; the price lives on the button; Robux and soft currency use distinct colours and icons; show a "worth" value beside discounted bundles.
- **Egg or crate shop:** odds line with a pity counter ("guaranteed legendary in N"), an item grid kept full with dim placeholder slots, a price bar, keybind action buttons (open, open 3, auto).
- **Daily or time rewards:** day tiles in a grid with the last day big and special; the full tile state set (ready, claimed, locked); timers on time-gated tiles; one pulsing claim; a gold premium skip.
- **Rebirth, prestige, upgrade:** a before and after row, a requirement bar with numbers, one line on what you keep versus lose, a primary green button and a gold skip.
- **Index or collection:** a grid with silhouettes or "?" for undiscovered items, a "5 / 20" count, tier filters in tier colours, a collection reward bar. Switch to ledger rows past about 40 items.
- **Ranks and tiers:** one emblem shape per ladder whose silhouette grows with the tier (base gem, then wings, then a crown); divisions by material colour; an outlined grey "unranked" emblem; always show the next tier and the points needed.
- **Wheel:** segments alternating 2 to 3 colours, labels along the radius, pointer at the top, +1 spin / spin / +5 buttons; ease out with a slight overshoot and a tick sound; odds list beside it.
