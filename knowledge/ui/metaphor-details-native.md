# Building screen-metaphor details with native UI

**Building the details with native UI**
- **Tape:** a small amber frame at about 0.15 transparency, rotated 35 to 45 degrees, half off the card corner.
- **Rubber stamp:** heavy red text with a red border stroke and small corner radius, rotated -6 to -10 degrees, slightly transparent.
- **Barcode:** a horizontal list of 20 to 30 thin black frames of varied width plus a caption.
- **Polaroid:** a white frame with more padding at the bottom, the image inside, a caption, rotated about 2 degrees, with a soft offset shadow.
- **Sticky note:** a yellow frame rotated about 3 degrees with a darker top band and an offset shadow.
- **CRT or monitor:** a dark rounded frame, a tiled scanline texture at 0.85 to 0.92 transparency, an inner glow, bright text.
- **Knob:** a circle with a stroke and a thin rotated line from the centre. **Speaker grille:** a grid of 3 px dots.
- **LCD counter:** a black inset with green digits in a pixel or monospace font and faint "88" ghost digits behind.
- **Gauge dial:** 20 to 30 small tick frames placed with sine and cosine from -120 to +120 degrees and rotated to match; colour the first k ticks.
- **Dashed line:** a list layout of short frames with padding.
- **Ticket card:** a dashed divider near one end and two half-circle notches in the background colour on the edges.
- Keep every decorative piece non-interactive, below content in ZIndex, and within about 60 decorative instances per window.
