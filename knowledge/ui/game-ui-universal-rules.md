# Universal rules of good Roblox game UI

1. **Colour means one thing everywhere.** Green = go, claim, buy with soft currency. Gold or orange = premium, skip, best value. Red = close, danger, limited. Blue = info or secondary. Purple = rare. Rainbow or animated gradient = top tier. Players learn it once and act without reading. Write the table down and use it on every button and tag.
2. **One primary action per screen,** biggest and brightest; secondary actions smaller or outlined; close always in the same corner (top right).
3. **Show the value, not just the price:** before and after rows with an arrow (x6 to x7), progress bars with "current / goal", countdowns, quantities on every reward, a "worth" label next to discounted bundles, capacity chips (used out of maximum, with a plus to expand).
4. **Every tile has visible states:** ready (glow, pulsing claim), claimed (dimmed with a check), locked (dark overlay, lock, time or requirement), selected (thick accent outline or corner brackets), equipped (check badge), new (red dot). Grids never look empty: pad unused cells with dim placeholder slots.
5. **Hierarchy by size:** the special item is bigger (the last daily tile spans two rows, the featured offer is a banner, the selected item gets a showcase panel).
6. **Tags pull attention** (NEW, LIMITED, BEST VALUE, SALE as small rotated pills; red dots on HUD buttons when something is claimable). Use them sparingly or they stop working.
7. **Break the frame:** a title, icon, character, close button or tab bar overlapping the panel edge makes a flat box feel alive.
