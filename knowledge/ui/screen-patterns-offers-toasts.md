# Screen patterns for offers, toasts, quests and menus

- **Price button:** currency icon plus amount; a "worth" label rotated slightly under it; a SALE ribbon whose gradient fades both ends; top-tier offers use a slowly animated rainbow gradient.
- **Offer or confirmation popup:** headline, the amount big with its icon, one line of context, the question with numbers highlighted, a green yes and a red no of equal size, close on the corner.
- **Currency fly-up:** reward icons spawn where earned, arc to the HUD counter with a trail, the counter ticks up and bumps (scale about 1.15), "+250" pops beside it. Pool the icons.
- **Toasts:** dark rounded pill, colourful icon left, short message with key words coloured, dismiss button, auto-hide after 3 to 4 s, at most 3 stacked.
- **Quests:** tab pills (all, daily, weekly), rows with progress and checks, a reward grid, claim all; optional thin HUD trackers.
- **Trading:** mirrored grids with a centre exchange icon, both names, green accept and red decline.
- **Main menu or loading:** a blurred or slowly drifting scene, big title, one obvious PLAY button, small news cards; a loading bar with a percentage and a skip button after a few seconds.
