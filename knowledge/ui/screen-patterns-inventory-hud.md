# Screen patterns for inventories, loadouts and stats

- **Showcase plus grid (inventory, armoury):** a big selected-item panel on one side, a filterable grid on the other, stats with bars, and one state button whose label changes (EQUIP, UPGRADE, MAXED).
- **Inventory with rarity tabs:** a tab bar of rarities on the top edge, search plus filter plus a capacity chip, a scrolling grid that fades out at the bottom, a detail panel that glows in the selected rarity colour (stat chips, XP bar, upgrade controls, equip), and a bulk action with a one-line hint.
- **Backpack and hotbar:** a bottom-centre hotbar of square slots mirrors the equipped items; the backpack window has search, a count, sort, "place best" and bulk equip, sell and unequip, with capacity upgrades beside it.
- **Loadouts:** a category rail, search, item grid with rarity borders, a detail panel, numbered loadout presets and "unequip all".
- **Stats screen:** a grid of stat cards (a coloured card with a dark inset holding the icon and value tinted in the card colour, caption on the coloured strip), beside a ranked list of rows with values right-aligned.
