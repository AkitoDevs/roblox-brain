# Universal UI rules: readability and consistency

8. **Readable on a phone:** chunky font with a dark stroke for short labels, at least about 14 px rendered text, tap targets about 44 px, abbreviated numbers (1.2K, 3.4M).
9. **Consistency is the style:** one corner radius, one stroke weight, one header recipe and one button recipe across all windows. A window's identity comes only from its header colour and icon.
10. **Motion is feedback:** buttons squash on press (0.94 to 0.96) and pop back; windows pop in; rewards fly to the counter, which ticks up and bumps; a glint sweeps across headers now and then.
11. **No pure black or white:** near-black ink and off-white read as designed, not default.
12. **Colour the one word that matters:** in a sentence, tint the key number or noun in its currency or rarity colour.
13. **Design each real screen for its job.** A swatch sheet of unrelated widgets side by side confuses people; preview a style on a real window, and say clearly when something is only a sample.
14. **Pick one style family for the frame system** and borrow only techniques from others, so the whole game reads as one system.
