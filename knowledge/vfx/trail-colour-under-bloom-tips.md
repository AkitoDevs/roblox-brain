# Coloured trails under bloom

Labelled **Unverified tip:** low-confidence report, not checked by us. See vfx/anatomy for the general colour rules.

- **Unverified tip:** in a bright map with bloom, a coloured cosmetic trail can render as a plain white streak.
- **Unverified tip:** lower LightEmission to roughly 0.35 to 0.5, and use a saturated three-stop colour sequence: light head, main colour, darker tail.
- Compare in the brightest place the trail will be used, not only a dark test area.
