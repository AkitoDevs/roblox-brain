# Game icons and thumbnails by genre

From a study of several hundred charting games' icons and thumbnails. No single image statistic predicted rank, so treat the numbers as a floor, not a formula: judge by eye and test with Roblox's thumbnail personalization.

**Pick the style by genre, then keep it across icon and thumbnails**
- RPG, anime, hero collectors: painted anime key art, or 3D unit renders.
- Fighting, sports, shooters and most simulators: a 3D render of characters or items.
- Obby and tycoon: bright cartoon with big numbers.
- Pet games: a mascot face.
- Survival and horror: dark and minimal (the only genre where dark wins).
- Shopping and roleplay: avatar fashion.
- Puzzle and board games: a flat single-object icon.
- Vehicle simulators: photoreal renders.

**What leading icons share**
- One subject (a face or eyes, or one object) filling about 60 to 85% of the frame, and it is the brightest, most saturated spot.
- One hot and one cold colour, with a complementary rim or glow.
- A title only when it helps recognition: heavy outline, at the top or bottom, readable at 150 px.
- 3 to 5 distinct thumbnails (a new boss, hero or moment) in the same style and palette family, update tags on fresh thumbnails, and the bottom ~15% of each thumbnail kept quiet.

**Floor checks before uploading (bottom-quartile mistakes)**
- Icon saturation at least 0.25 (aim around 0.5) and luminance at least 0.22, unless the genre is horror, where dark needs high saturation (0.65+) plus a bright accent.
- Enough contrast to read at 64 px.
- Thumbnails not desaturated; more than one thumbnail; icon and thumbnails share a hero and palette.
- No placeholder tiles, no tiny subject lost in a big scene, no tagline text.

**Practicalities:** icons at least 512 px square, previewed at 150 and 64 px; thumbnails 16:9 at 1920 × 1080 and under 3 MB. For personalization tests, run 2 to 5 variants, wait about 7 days (or until the next update) before restarting, don't test near-copies, vary gameplay, character, environment or motivation, and judge by play-through rate rather than clicks. Claimed click-rate gains from vendors are unverified.
