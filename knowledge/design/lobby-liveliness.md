# Making a lobby or hub feel alive

**Why lobbies feel dull:** a big empty floor and ceiling, features scattered with no framework, unlabeled features, flat uniform light, nothing moving, and boards stuck on placeholder text that reads as broken.

**What fixes it:**
1. **Clear layout.** One focal centre (arena, stage, plaza) that players gather around, with feature districts around it, each on a clear path. Keep the spawn's sightline to the centre open.
2. **Big readable signs per area.** Bold display font, thick dark outline, a backlit or glowing board, mounted high enough to read across the room, all in one shared style.
3. **One pad language.** Every usable thing gets the same marker: a glowing ring in the area's accent colour, a floating label with an icon, a soft light. Players learn it once.
4. **Characters everywhere.** An NPC with a nameplate in each area (with a "!" when something is ready), units on glowing pedestals, leaderboard winners as statues, and a designed stand-in (a veiled statue) when a spot is unclaimed.
5. **Dress the edges.** Every edge, corner and gap gets a prop cluster; keep walkways and the centre clear. No bare stretch of floor over about 20 studs without a reason.
6. **Use the ceiling.** Banners, chandeliers, trusses, floating decor, wall screens.
7. **Theatrical light.** A calmer base with strong accents; each area owns one accent colour (uplights, neon edge strips, spots on signs and statues).
8. **Floor guidance.** Paths with arrows or chevrons toward the main activity, ideally pulsing in sequence.
9. **Live screens.** Countdowns, boss timers, leaderboards, news. Wire every number to real server data; placeholder numbers kill the effect.
10. **Constant gentle motion.** Swaying banners, rotating emblems, low-rate particles, bobbing objects, idle NPCs. Something should move in every view.
11. **Event hooks.** Swappable seasonal decor, and timed visitors (a travelling merchant every couple of hours) that give players a reason to come back.
12. **Identity first.** Express all of the above in the game's own theme; never reuse another game's sign wording, mascots, colours or layout.

**Prices in the lobby** should scale with the player's progress but keep the multiplier small; check the cost for a brand-new player and for an endgame account before shipping.
