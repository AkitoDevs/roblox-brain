# RNG game maps, menus and daily systems

**The map as a stage.** A flat, wide plaza so crowds of auras don't overlap, raised walkways for paths, seats to sit and watch, tall leaderboard boards with the top three coloured, interactables with a bright frame and a prompt.

**Menus.** One chassis everywhere: a dark translucent panel with thin borders and corner brackets, a centred title bar with close, a detail or preview side plus a searchable list or grid. Outlined buttons whose text matches the outline colour (green equip, red delete, blue lock, gold upgrade). Show drop chances for every chest with names in rarity colours. Locked settings show a large "disabled" label over the row instead of being hidden.

**Collection showroom:** a plain mannequin on a grid floor, the full list with locked entries, a detail card (name, odds, tier, biome conditions, a flavour line), and replay-cutscene and hide-UI buttons.

**Daily systems:** a 7-day login row with a countdown, daily quests plus a quest for finishing all of them, achievements at roll milestones, a season pass with free and premium tracks and a claim-all.

**First session:** dim everything except the one control to use next, give a two-line instruction in the centre, and step through roll, quests, claim and inventory.

The same pattern fits hero and pet summon games: growing spectacle by tier, the star-flare reveal for top pulls, visible odds, skip thresholds and swap warnings.
