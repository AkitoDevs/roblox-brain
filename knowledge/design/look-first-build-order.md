# Look first, detail last: scene build order

**Look first, detail last**
1. Blockout in neutral grey: big shapes, framing, a path to the focal point, three depth planes. Openings are real holes, because a solid "window" blocks motivated light. Streets and alleys get stepped facades of different heights and depths; parallel walls of one height read as a corridor.
2. Light the blockout: a key light, practicals with a visible source (a flame or bulb you can see, not a hidden light), Atmosphere, a grade.
3. One mood shot for approval before any detailing. Judge the mood first, before any numbers: value numbers can pass while the mood is wrong. A grey blockout can't show warmth or a toy-like feel, so judge those after colour.
4. Then assets, ground dressing, motion and UI, in that order.

**Review in one batch:** several angles plus a greyscale capture, checking mood, value (the focal point has the strongest contrast), real shadows, spacing and negative space, a restrained palette, silhouette, and that every requirement is visible within seconds of spawning. Fix everything found in one batch, confirm once, then hand it over for a human eye.
