# Greyscale, lighting and critique checks for builds

**Materials and light, checked in greyscale**
- One recipe per material: geometry, several hues and a wear logic.
- A key light with a believable source. Then a greyscale check of which shape wins: the focal point should be the value peak and the darks should stay readable. A bright foreground block can steal the focal point.
- Every light has a source: sourceless glows and cones read fake and compete with the focal point.
- Haze tuned to a number flattens form; mood comes from the key light and value structure.
- Effects and details stay only if they read in the capture; cut unseen beams and shafts.
- **Day, dusk and night pass:** keep the world's hues fixed and change only value and fog colour; keep warm lights (windows, lanterns) the same colour at all three so they gain contrast as the world darkens. Match fog to the horizon sky colour so far shapes fade toward it, not toward black. Judge the scene by the three captures, not one.
- Motion (clouds, dust, particles) is judged in motion, never from stills.
