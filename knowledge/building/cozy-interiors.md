# Cozy, lived-in interiors

**Why it works**
1. **Small rooms, packed with life.** About one bed or table wide on each side of a walkway, every surface holding something. Small rooms feel intimate and cost few parts.
2. **Everyday objects with a story:** an unmade bed with a patchwork quilt and a plush on the pillow, an old TV with a box on top, a steaming pot on the stove, bread on a cutting board, a single flower in a vase on a table runner, moving boxes in the hall, framed prints leaning against the wall, a notice board covered in notes.
3. **Dark rooms, bright windows.** A fairly dark interior and a very bright outside, so windows glow and the sun throws shafts and window-shaped pools onto beds and floors. That contrast is most of the mood.
4. **One colour grade:** a warm tint pushed slightly green-yellow, deep shadows, slightly lifted contrast, like a film still.
5. **Seasonal touches** (glowing carved pumpkins that light the wall behind them, seasonal posters) keep the house feeling current.

**Every room** has a rug, at least one warm lamp, a plant, wall art, a light switch by the door, and a skirting or trim line where wall meets floor. Bedrooms: patterned blankets, floor cushions, a nightstand lamp, sheer curtains. Hallways: balusters, panelled doors with brass handles, a mirror over a dresser, a coat rack, wainscoting. Kitchens: upper and lower cabinets with handles, a tiled backsplash, a range hood, a utensil rail, a dining table with a runner.
