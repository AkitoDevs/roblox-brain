# Signpost arrow tip tips

Labelled **Unverified tip:** because it is one developer's report and has not been checked by us. Low value, but a cheap way to avoid a crude look.

- **Unverified tip:** a point made from a square part turned 45 degrees reads as a separate diamond stuck on the board. Use two mirrored wedges instead, one above and one below the board's middle line.
- **Unverified tip:** size each wedge as board thickness by half the board height by the tip length. Turn it so its right-angle corner sits on the middle line at the board's end.
- **Unverified tip:** confirm the triangle is clean by casting rays through the tip corners.
