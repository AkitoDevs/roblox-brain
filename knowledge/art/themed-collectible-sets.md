# Themed collectible sets that read as one family

For catalogues of collectibles in themed sets: eggs, pets, chests, summon capsules, crates.

**Why good sets work**
1. **One base form for the whole catalogue:** same shape, same size, so items line up in a shop row and read instantly as "an egg".
2. **One signature focal point** in the same spot on every item, such as a glowing gem on the front. It is the brightest thing on the model, its colour marks the set, and the rest of the design points at it (cracks radiate from it, chevrons frame it, stripes curve around it).
3. **The creature lives in the details:** ears, horns, a crown of spikes, tusks, a snout, markings, small clothing. The top outline alone should tell you which creature it is from the front.
4. **Each set has a material language.** Fire: dark basalt with glowing cracks from the core, spikes shading dark red to yellow at the tips. Jungle: real fur colours, a leafy garland, a sprout on top. Blossom: shrine-style trim with small flowers scattered over the surface. Ice: snow caps over three blues, icicles dripping from every ledge, a crystal crown.
5. **Few colours, stepped shading:** 3 to 5 colours per item plus the glow, each with 2 to 3 shades forming clean bands, not noise.
6. **A clear value plan:** a dark body with bright glows, or a light body with saturated accents. Never mid-tones on mid-tones.
7. **Grounded:** base details (leaf clumps, a stone ring, snow) make the item sit instead of float.
8. **Rarity through ornament** on the same base form: crowns, bigger horns, more glowing cracks, more trim.
